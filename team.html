Quality

How a part earns its release.

Every build follows the same route. It is deliberately boring — the interesting failures happen when a step gets skipped.

01

Source before shape

No part is built from a product photo or a marketplace listing. We work from the manufacturer datasheet, package drawing and current revision — and record which one we used.

02

Independent review

A second engineer checks pad geometry, mask and paste, pin-1 orientation and 3D body clearance. The builder cannot sign off their own footprint.

03

One source of truth

Symbol, footprint and part record are linked by a single part number. Change one, and the revision trail shows what else needs attention.

04

Traceable by design

Every delivery includes a change log and review notes, so an auditor, a new engineer or your future self can see exactly what was checked and when.
