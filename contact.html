What to send us

  1. A part list or datasheets — PDFs are fine.
  2. Your CAD tool and version, plus any template or library rules.
  3. Density preference (A, B or C) and the assembly process.
  4. Deadline, board volume and whether rework access matters.

What happens after you hit send.

No discovery maze. Most quotes are turned around the same day, and a sample part can be built before you commit to a batch.

01 · Brief review We read the part list, check the CAD tool and flag anything missing before quoting — so the number you get is the number you pay.
02 · Scope & quote A fixed per-part price, a review fee where it applies, and a delivery date. Datasheet gaps are listed explicitly.
03 · Sample & release We build one representative part first. Once you approve the standard, the rest of the batch follows the same checklist.
